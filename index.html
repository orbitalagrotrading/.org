Ratnagiri Alphonso

The Hapus: golden skin with a red blush, saffron-orange pulp and no fibre. Konkan Alphonso carries a Geographical Indication tag.

  • Season: March to June
  • Hand-picked, naturally ripened
  • Boxed in straw, by the dozen or the crate

Devrukh cashews

Konkan cashews, roasted and shelled in the region where the trees grow along the coast. Crisp, sweet and buttery.

  • Whole kernels and pieces
  • Sealed for freshness
  • Available all year

Chiku

Sapota with soft, honey-brown flesh and a malty sweetness. Eat it fresh, blend it into milkshakes or chill it as a dessert.

  • Picked at eating-ripe stage
  • Best in the cooler months
  • Sold by the kilo

Fresh buffalo milk

Thick, creamy and rich in fat, so it makes better dahi, paneer, ghee and chai. Collected daily from local farms.

  • Delivered fresh each morning
  • No additives
  • Order by the litre

Green hills, red laterite soil and a long coastline

Ratnagiri's warm coast, heavy monsoon and mineral-rich earth give its fruit and nuts their character.
